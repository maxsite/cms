# Security Policy

**Effective Date:** September 6, 2026

## Support Status: END-OF-LIFE / UNMAINTAINED

This project is no longer actively maintained by the development team in terms of security. We do not guarantee that any reported vulnerabilities will be fixed, and we do not participate in any responsible disclosure process.

## Rules

- **No Bounties:** We do not offer any financial or non‑financial rewards for discovering vulnerabilities.
- **No Acknowledgment:** We will not confirm, deny, or respond to private reports about potential security issues. Your name will not appear in any contributors’ list or release notes.
- **Public Disclosure:** If you find a vulnerability, you may publish it only if the project has been officially archived, or at your own risk. We do not grant permission to release working exploits for active installations.

## Liability

Any use of discovered vulnerabilities against end‑user systems without explicit consent is illegal and violates applicable laws. The project owner disclaims all liability for any damage, data loss, or harm caused by third‑party exploitation of any flaws in this software.

## Contact

Please do not send emails or issues regarding security findings. Such messages will be ignored and deleted without a reply.
